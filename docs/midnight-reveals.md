# Midnight reveals
- Every wheel or directly drawn forfeit is provisional until Accept. Decline leaves uses and unlocks unchanged.
- Double/Triple retain the original as a pending first slot. Replacements discard it without applying its effects.
- Devil’s Five reserves up to five different eligible results from one randomly chosen group. The queue, accepted/declined states, modifier choice and timers survive reload.
- Reveal next is the default. Editor autoplay reveals the next slot after a decision; it never accepts automatically.
- Sealed Envelopes is a configurable Fate card (set its copies above zero). Offer two or three unique eligible results, optional group or base-duration hints, and an optional group pool. Choosing discards the other envelopes without effects.
- Pause freezes batch timers. Reload resumes the same selection. End remaining reveals declines unresolved slots. Done pauses remaining batch timers before closing.
- Ordinary timed results are accepted before their timer can start. The accepted result and deadline survive reload.
- Card illustrations are local SVGs with no image replacement observer. Original PNG artwork remains in the repository but is not requested by the play page.
- Graphics use a midnight palette, a centered quiet wheel, consistent card frames, a central reveal stage, chapter progress and collapsible history.
- Existing XML remains compatible. New settings are in fateDeck and reveals.
